Frequently Asked Questions
Everything you need to know before getting started.
What markets does AG Technicals cover? ↓
We cover Forex (all major & minor pairs), Gold & Silver, Bitcoin & Crypto, Indian Markets (NSE/BSE), Indices (NAS100, SPX500, US30) and Crude Oil. If it moves, we have analysis on it.
Is this financial advice? ↓
No. AG Technicals provides market analysis, educational content, and tools only. Nothing on this platform constitutes financial advice. All trading decisions are your own responsibility. Please read the full risk disclaimer below.
How do the TV Indicators work? ↓
Our custom TradingView indicators (AG SMC, AG Order Flow, AG-ESB) are Pine Script-based scripts that get added directly to your TradingView chart. Once set up, they auto-detect key levels, signals, and zones in real time. Setup takes under 5 minutes — we guide you through it.
What is the Bridge / copy trading? ↓
The Bridge connects your broker account to AG Technicals's signal source. When a trade fires on our end, it automatically executes on your MT4/MT5 account with your chosen lot size and risk settings. No manual intervention needed.
Do I need trading experience to use AG Technicals? ↓
Not necessarily. Our Education and Guide sections are built for beginners, while our indicators and algo tools suit experienced traders. We have products for every level — if you're unsure where to start, message us on WhatsApp.
How do I get access to indicators or the algo? ↓
Click the relevant product button (TV Indicator, Bridge, or Algo) on this page, then tap "Get this on WhatsApp." We'll confirm availability, explain setup, and handle everything from there.
What is the win rate and how is it measured? ↓
Our stated win rate is based on tracked signals across live accounts over multiple months. A win is counted when the trade hits TP1 before stop loss. We publish our track record publicly — ask us for the latest stats on WhatsApp.
