General Settings 5 settings

EA-wide master switches — top-level ON/OFF, auto-trading, the Detection TF ("Master Stroke"), OTE mode, and minimum confluence required to trade.

CONTROLS EVERYTHING
How a trade fires here

This topic never fires a trade itself. MasterEnable is the top switch — off means nothing runs at all, anywhere in the EA. MinConfluenceToTrade sets the bar every other topic's score is checked against.

Extra Precaution

Setting MinConfluenceToTrade too low (e.g. 1) with many topics ON means almost any single signal can trigger a trade — combine it with the Recommended Combinations chapter rather than leaving it at the default while running everything.

SettingDefaultUse
MasterEnabletrueTurn the whole EA fully on or off. When off, nothing runs at all — no visuals, no trades.
EnableAutoTradingtrueTurn ON when you want the EA to actually place trades, not just draw zones on the chart.
EA_DetectionTFTRADER MINDSETPERIOD_CURRENTLeave on Current for normal use. Set to a higher timeframe (e.g. H1 on an M15 chart) for bigger, cleaner swing points while still trading on your chart's own timeframe.
OTERequireTrendConfirmationfalseKeep ON for a simpler single OTE zone that only trades with the trend. Turn OFF for the advanced dual-zone standalone mode.
MinConfluenceToTradeTRADER MINDSET1Higher = fewer but stronger trades (multiple signals must line up). Lower = more frequent trades on weaker signals.

Swing High/Low 3 settings

Fractal-based swing point detection — a candle counts as a swing high/low once enough candles on both sides confirm it. Every other topic reads its price levels FROM these swing points.

VISUAL ONLY
How a trade fires here

Swing High/Low never fires a trade and never adds to the confluence score. It is the foundation every other topic reads its price levels from — BOS, CHoCH, OB, FVG, PD, and OTE all depend on it indirectly.

Extra Precaution

If LeftBars/RightBars are set very high, swings become rare — every downstream topic will also fire less often, since they're all waiting on these same swing points.

SettingDefaultUse
LeftBars2How many candles to the left must be smaller/bigger for a swing point to count. Higher = fewer, more significant swings.
RightBars2How many candles to the right must confirm the swing before it's accepted. Higher = more reliable but more delayed.
ShowSwingPointstrueShow/hide the small arrows marking each confirmed swing high/low on the chart.

BOS (Break of Structure) 3 settings

Price closes beyond the last swing point in the SAME direction as the existing trend — confirms the trend is continuing.

CONTRIBUTES TO SCORE
How a trade fires here

A fresh BOS (this bar only) adds +1 to the confluence score if TradeOnBOS is ON. It never fires a trade by itself — it needs enough OTHER signals to also agree before MinConfluenceToTrade is reached.

Extra Precaution

BOS confirms an EXISTING trend is continuing — it will not catch reversals. Pair it with CHoCH if you want the EA to also react to trend flips.

SettingDefaultUse
EnableBOSfalseTurn BOS detection on or off entirely.
ShowBOStrueShow/hide the BOS line and label on the chart.
TradeOnBOStrueIf ON, a fresh BOS adds +1 to the confluence score.

Price closes beyond the last swing point AGAINST the existing trend — the same break mechanism as BOS, but it flips the trend.

CONTRIBUTES TO SCORE

Extra Precaution

CONTRIBUTES TO SCORE
How a trade fires here

Price returning into a valid OB (or Breaker) zone adds +1 to the confluence score if TradeOnOB/TradeOnBreaker is ON. Never fires alone — it's one vote among several.

Extra Precaution

OBEntryMode = Touch reacts to a wick alone, even if the candle closes back outside the zone — this catches more setups but also more false ones than Close mode.

FVG (Fair Value Gap) 11 settings

A 3-candle price imbalance — one side of the middle candle never traded, leaving a gap. PRECISE FVG filters out weak/fake gaps.

CONTRIBUTES TO SCORE
How a trade fires here

Price retesting an unfilled FVG adds +1 to the confluence score if TradeOnFVG is ON. Never fires a trade alone.

Extra Precaution

With PreciseFVG off, weak gaps left by low-conviction candles count the same as strong displacement gaps — turning it on (default) filters these out, at the cost of slightly fewer signals.

SettingDefaultUse
EnableFVGfalseTurn FVG detection on or off entirely.
AutoDeleteFVGtrueIf ON, a gap is automatically removed once price has fully filled it.
FVGCount3Maximum number of FVG zones kept per direction — smallest ones drop off first.
FVGEntryModeOB_ON_CLOSEClose = price must CLOSE inside the gap. Touch = a wick touching the gap is enough.
RequireFVGSwingRetestTRADER MINDSETtrueIf ON, requires a small internal swing to form INSIDE the gap before it counts — filters out gaps price just blasted through.
FVGSwingLeftBars1Left-bar setting for that internal tight swing check.
FVGSwingRightBars1Right-bar setting for that internal tight swing check.
PreciseFVGTRADER MINDSETtruePRECISE FVG — requires the middle candle to have a strong, decisive body, filtering out weak/fake gaps.
PreciseFVGMinBodyPercent60.0How strong (as % of candle range) that middle candle's body must be. Higher = stricter, fewer but cleaner gaps.
FVGColorRuleFVG_COLOR_ANYHow many of the 3 candles must agree in color before the gap counts — Any / 2-of-3 / All 3.
FVGActionOnCHoCHFVG_KEEP_FOR_REUSEWhat happens to opposite-direction FVGs on a CHoCH — keep them for later, or delete them immediately.

Inducement (IDM) 3 settings

A small internal swing gets swept — a LEADING signal that can fire before the main trend structure confirms.

CONTRIBUTES TO SCOREFIRES OWN TRADE
How a trade fires here

Normal IDM adds +1 to the confluence score if TradeOnIDM is ON — one vote among several. BUT if IDM_REVERSAL_ENTRY is switched ON, that specific feature fires its OWN independent trade the very next candle after any IDM event, based purely on that candle's color — completely bypassing the confluence score.

Extra Precaution

IDM_REVERSAL_ENTRY only requires MinConfluenceToTrade ≤ 1 to activate, regardless of how high you've set it elsewhere. If you don't want this standalone behavior, keep IDM_REVERSAL_ENTRY switched off.

TradeOnIDMtrueIf ON, a fresh IDM event adds +1 to the confluence score.
IDM_REVERSAL_ENTRYTRADER MINDSETfalseTurns on a standalone trade path: the candle right after any IDM event decides trade direction by its own color, independent of the main trend.

Premium & Discount (PD) Zones 4 settings

A % zone measured from each swing extreme — Discount near the swing low for buys, Premium near the swing high for sells.

CONTRIBUTES TO SCORE
How a trade fires here

Price sitting in the matching Premium/Discount zone adds +1 to the confluence score if TradeOnPD is ON. The zones themselves never fire a trade directly — that's the Grid Basket's job (next chapter).

Extra Precaution

PDZonePercent too large makes Premium and Discount overlap the middle of the range, so almost any price counts as "in zone" — keep it tight (20–35%) for the zones to mean something.

PDExtendBars15How far forward (in candles) the PD boxes are drawn.

Places several pending orders across the PD zone. All legs share ONE take-profit, computed from the basket's average filled price.

FIRES OWN TRADE
How a trade fires here

This is a fully standalone mechanism, separate from confluence scoring. The moment a new PD zone forms, EnablePDGrid automatically places PDGridTrades pending limit orders across it — no score check, no MinConfluenceToTrade gate.

Extra Precaution

This is a grid/DCA-style approach: multiple entries, one shared averaged take-profit. It behaves very differently from every other topic in this EA — test it in isolation before combining it with other live topics.

PDMaxConsecutiveSLStopTRADER MINDSET1After this many stop-losses in a row on a zone, re-entry is permanently blocked for it (0 = never blocks).

OTE (Optimal Trade Entry) 9 settings

A fibonacci retracement zone — the classic 61.8%–79% "sweet spot" pullback area.

CONTRIBUTES TO SCOREFIRES OWN TRADE
How a trade fires here

In trend-confirmed mode (OTERequireTrendConfirmation = true), price in the OTE zone adds to the confluence score (weighted by OTEWeight) — one vote among several. In Standalone mode (false), OTE fires its OWN independent trade the moment price enters either zone, completely bypassing trend and confluence.

Extra Precaution

Standalone mode keeps BOTH buy and sell OTE zones live at once. In a choppy market this can produce a buy signal and a sell signal within the same session — review OTEZoneMode (Both/Buy-only/Sell-only) if that's not what you want.

SettingDefaultUse
EnableOTEfalseTurn OTE (fibonacci) zones on or off.
OTEFibStart0.618Where the OTE zone starts, as a fibonacci retracement level (e.g. 0.618).
OTEFibEnd0.79Where the OTE zone ends, as a fibonacci retracement level (e.g. 0.79).
OTEEntryModeOB_ON_TOUCHClose = price must CLOSE inside the zone. Touch = a wick touching it is enough.
OTEAllowReentrySameZoneTRADER MINDSETfalseIf ON, more than one trade can be taken on the same locked OTE zone.
OTEMaxSessionsPerZoneTRADER MINDSET1Maximum number of re-entries allowed on the same locked zone.
OTEMaxConsecutiveSLStopTRADER MINDSET1After this many stop-losses in a row on a side, re-entry is permanently blocked for it (0 = never blocks).
OTESLShiftTRADER MINDSETfalseIf ON, uses a tighter internal swing for Stop Loss when one is available, instead of always using the full zone extreme.
OTEZoneModeTRADER MINDSETOTE_BOTH_ZONESStandalone mode only — trade Both Zones, Only the Buy zone, or Only the Sell zone.

An independent confluence contributor — compares your chart's own trend against the combined bias of four higher timeframes.

CONTRIBUTES TO SCORE

Zone Re-Entry Limit 3 settings

Caps how many times the SAME zone (OB or FVG) is allowed to fire a main trade, with an optional permanent block after repeated stop-losses.

BLOCKS TRADES
How a trade fires here

This topic never fires a trade — it can only PREVENT one. Once a specific OB/FVG zone has produced MaxTradesPerZone trades, or hit ZoneMaxConsecutiveSL losses in a row, that zone is quietly blocked from firing again, even if every other confluence condition is met.

Extra Precaution

A trade that matched TWO zone types at once (e.g. an OB and an FVG together) is only attributed to the FIRST one checked (OB takes priority) — this is an approximation, not a perfectly precise per-zone count.

SettingDefaultUse

ZoneMaxConsecutiveSLTRADER MINDSET0After this many stop-losses in a row from one zone, that zone is permanently blocked (0 = never blocks). A clean win resets the count.

SL (Stop Loss) Method 9 settings

How Stop Loss is measured for every trade — swing-based or a fixed point distance.

SHAPES SIZE & RISK
How a trade fires here

This topic never fires or blocks a trade — it only decides WHERE the stop-loss sits once a trade has already been triggered by everything else.

Extra Precaution

EnableSL = false sends NO stop-loss to the broker at all. This is extremely high risk and should only be used if you fully understand — and accept — that a trade could run against you with no automatic exit.

SettingDefaultUse
EnableSLTRADER MINDSETtrueMaster switch for Stop Loss. Turning this off sends NO stop loss to the broker — trades run fully unprotected. Extremely high risk; leave on unless you fully understand the consequences.
ZoneMatchTolerancePointsTRADER MINDSET50Small tolerance (in points) added around every zone so near-misses still count as a match.
SLMethodSL_SWINGSwing = SL placed at the relevant swing point. Fixed Points = SL always a fixed distance from entry.
SwingSLShiftTRADER MINDSETfalseIf ON (and SLMethod = Swing), the SL starts at the freshest swing AND keeps trailing toward newer swings for as long as the trade stays open — always tightening, never loosening.
FixedSLPoints500The fixed SL distance used when SLMethod = Fixed Points.
UseMaxSLDistanceTRADER MINDSETfalseIf ON, skips a trade entirely when the calculated SL would be too far away.
MaxSLDistancePointsTRADER MINDSET20000The maximum SL distance allowed before a trade is skipped.

Recommended Combinations Read Before Going Live

Which topics work well switched on together, and why.

Recommended Topic Combinations

Turning topics ON in logical groups tends to work better than switching on everything at once, or trading a single topic in isolation. A few groupings that fit together well by design:

1. Core Structure Group

BOS + CHoCH + Order Block + FVG — a classic trend-following setup. Order Block and FVG both depend on the trend direction, so pairing them with BOS/CHoCH (which set that direction) is a natural fit.

2. Liquidity / Manipulation Group

EQH/EQL + Liquidity Sweep + Inducement (IDM) — both detect liquidity grabs. IDM is a leading signal (can fire before trend structure confirms); Sweep is more reactive. Used together they aim to catch stop-hunt / fake-out moves before the real move starts.

3. Precise Entry Group

Premium & Discount + OTE — both are zone-based entries (PD is a wider zone, OTE a tighter fibonacci zone). Add these ON TOP OF the Core Structure Group rather than running them alone — without trend context from BOS/CHoCH, a PD/OTE zone match loses meaning.

4. Confirmation Layer

Multi-Timeframe (MTF) — safe to add to any combination above. It only adds an extra "higher-timeframe agreement" check; it never fires a trade by itself.

IMPORTANT NOTE

There is no single "best" combination that works for every instrument, timeframe, and market condition. The groupings above describe how the LOGIC connects internally — they are a sensible starting point, not a guarantee of performance.

Before trading any combination live, always test it first in the MetaTrader Strategy Tester on your own instrument and timeframe, over a meaningful history of data. Results differ significantly across symbols (e.g. Gold vs a Forex pair) and timeframes.

Also note: the more topics you switch ON, the higher MinConfluenceToTrade should generally be set — otherwise weak, low-confidence signals can trigger trades too easily. Standalone paths (IDM_REVERSAL_ENTRY, OTE Standalone mode, the PD Grid Basket) sit OUTSIDE these groupings entirely — they fire their own independent trades and should be tested separately from the confluence-based combinations above.
