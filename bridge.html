How It Works
4 Steps To Full Automation
01
Signal Fires
Your TradingView strategy or alert fires — buy or sell, which symbol, at what price.
02
Cloud Validates
AG Bridge server receives the webhook, validates the signal — correct format, correct secret — then queues it for your license.
03
EA Picks Up
The MT5 Expert Advisor polls the server every few seconds and picks up the waiting signal instantly.
04
Trade Executes
The trade opens on your MT5 account. The EA manages stop loss, take profit, and break-even automatically.
