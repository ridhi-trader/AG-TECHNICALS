Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Digital Products

AG Technicals sells digital software and educational content: MT5 Expert Advisors (EAs), TradingView indicators, licences and subscriptions, and custom development work. Because these are delivered digitally and cannot be returned, payments are generally non-refundable once the product, licence or access has been delivered or activated.

2. When We Will Refund

We will refund your payment in full in these cases:

  • Duplicate payment: you were charged more than once for the same order.
  • Not delivered: payment was successful but we could not deliver the product, licence or access, and we are unable to fix it within a reasonable time.
  • Technical defect: the product does not work as described and our support team cannot resolve it after reasonable attempts.

Refunds are not given for a change of mind, for trading losses, or because results differ from your expectations. Trading outcomes depend on market conditions and your own decisions, and AG Technicals does not promise any profit.

3. Subscriptions & Cancellation

You can stop a subscription or licence from renewing at any time by contacting us before the renewal date. Access continues until the end of the period you have already paid for. Amounts for a period that has already started are not refunded.

4. Custom Development

For custom indicator or algo work, you can cancel before work begins for a full refund. Once development has started, we may keep a fair portion of the fee for the work already done. After the finished tool has been delivered, the payment is non-refundable.

5. How To Request A Refund

Email support@agtechnicals.com or message us on WhatsApp or Telegram within 7 days of the payment. Please include your name, the email or phone number used for the order, the payment reference or transaction ID, and a short description of the problem.

6. Refund Timeline

Once a refund is approved, we process it to the original payment method. It usually reaches your account within 5–7 working days, depending on your bank or payment provider.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy: support@agtechnicals.com. See also our Terms of Service and Risk Disclaimer.
