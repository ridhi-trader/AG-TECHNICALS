Section 01 / 13 — AG Technicals Swing + EMA + R:R EA

Introduction Overview

Is chapter mein hum samjhenge ki AG Swing + EMA + R:R EA kya hai, aur ye kaise kaam karta hai.

1. EA Kya Hai

AG Swing + EMA + R:R EA (Expert Advisor) ek full auto-trading MT5 bot hai — jo humare Swing + EMA logic ke saath khud entries leta hai, SL/TP manage karta hai, aur position ko fully automatic control karta hai. Ye real trades place karta hai, real partial close karta hai, aur poora trade management khud handle karta hai — bina kisi manual intervention ke.

2. Core Logic Same Hai

EA ka core signal logic 2 cheezon pe based hai:

  • EMA — trend direction batata hai
  • Swing High / Swing Low break — momentum confirmation deta hai

Jab dono conditions match hoti hain, EA khud BUY ya SELL order place kar deta hai.

EA Ke Full Feature Set: Master Control (On/Off, Only Buy/Sell), 3 Lot Sizing modes (Fixed/% Risk/$ Risk), Real Partial Close (actual lot close hota hai), EMA Timeframe (higher timeframe trend filter), aur AG Technicals branded dashboard.

3. Kis Liye Bana Hai

Un traders ke liye jo apna trading system fully automate karna chahte hain — bina manually chart dekhe, bina manually order place kiye. EA khud signal detect karta hai, risk calculate karta hai, aur trade execute karta hai.

EMA Basics Exponential Moving Average

EMA kya hai aur EA mein iska role kya hai.

1. EMA Kya Hai

EMA (Exponential Moving Average) ek moving average hai jo recent candles ko zyada weight deta hai — isliye price ke sath fast react karta hai. Ye ek smooth trend line hai jo batati hai overall trend kis direction mein hai.

Swing High / Low Break Concept

Swing detect karne ke 2 tarike — Normal aur Basic.

1. Swing High / Low Kya Hai

Swing High wo candle hoti hai jiske dono taraf ke candles usse neeche hon (local peak). Swing Low iska ulta — dono taraf ke candles usse upar hon.

Swing points chart pe "SWH" / "SWL" chip-box ke saath automatically mark hote hain

2. Do Calculation Types (ENUM)

TypeKaise Kaam Karta Hai
NormalSwing Lookback Length parameter use karta hai — jitna zyada, utni door/major swing (wide SL)
BasicFixed 2 candle left / 2 candle right classic fractal — jyada frequent, nazdeeki swings (tighter SL)
Kaunsa Choose Karein: Agar SL bahut wide lag raha hai, Basic type try karo — chhoti, jaldi milne wali swings deta hai. Agar zyada reliable/major levels chahiye, Normal rakho aur Lookback Length adjust karo.

3. "Break" Ka Matlab

Candle close se pichhla swing high/low cross hona chahiye — wick se nahi. Isse fake wick spikes se bachav hota hai.

BUY / SELL Logic Core Signal

Jab dono conditions match hon, EA khud order place karta hai.

1. Signal Kab Fire Hota Hai

BBUY

SSELL
1. Candle close EMA ke neeche
2. AND close previous Swing Low ko break kare
3. → EA turant SELL order place karta hai

2. Chart Pe Visual

Risk:Reward + Partial Close Real Trade Management

Real trade management — asli partial position close hoti hai, sirf visual nahi.

1. R:R Kaise Kaam Karta Hai

Har trade ke saath Entry, SL, aur 3 Take-Profit levels calculate hote hain — R (Risk) multiple ke basis pe. R = Entry se SL tak ka distance.

Chart pe Entry/SL/TP1/TP2/TP3 sabki ray-lines automatically draw hoti hain, symbol ke saath

2. Do TP Modes

ModeKaise Kaam Karta Hai
Real Partial CloseTP1 pe position ka X% actually close hota hai, TP2 pe aur close, baaki TP3 pe. Real lot reduction hoti hai.
Breakeven-TrailPartial close nahi hota — TP1 hit hone pe SL breakeven pe move hota hai, TP2 pe SL TP1 tak trail hota hai. Poori position TP3 tak chalti hai.
Kaunsa Choose Karein: Real Partial Close agar profit lock karna chahte ho jaldi. Breakeven-Trail agar bade moves capture karne hain poori position ke saath, bas risk-free karna hai jaldi.

3. Settings

ParameterKya Karta HaiDefault
R:R Master Enablefalse karo to koi TP nahi lagega, trade sirf SL pe chalegatrue
TP1/TP2/TP3 R MultipleHar TP kitne R door ho1 / 2 / 3
Partial Close Master Enablefalse karo to full close sirf final TP pe hogatrue
TP ModeReal Partial Close / Breakeven-TrailReal Partial Close

EMA Timeframe Multi-Timeframe Trend

Chhote chart pe entries lo, bade timeframe ke trend ke saath.

1. Concept

Normally EMA usi timeframe se calculate hota hai jis chart pe EA lagi hai. Lekin ab EMA ka timeframe alag se choose kar sakte ho — jaise M5 chart pe trade lo, lekin trend-filter H1 EMA se lo.

Bada trend (H1) same rahe jab tak, chhoti TF (M5) pe multiple entries lagti reh sakti hain (Max Trade Row limit ke andar)

2. Kyun Useful Hai

  • Higher TF EMA se overall trend confirm hota hai — chhoti TF ki fake reversals filter ho jaati hain
  • M5 pe TP hit hone ke baad bhi, jab tak H1 trend wahi hai, EA dobara same direction mein entry le sakta hai
  • Strong trending moves mein zyada capture karta hai jo single-trade approach miss kar deta
Dhyan Rahe: Swing High/Low detection hamesha chart ke apne timeframe pe rehta hai (MTF nahi hota) — sirf EMA trend-filter higher TF se aata hai. Higher TF EMA value tab tak "stale" rehti hai jab tak uska bada candle close na ho jaye (M5 chart pe H1 EMA — value har ghante hi update hogi).
Max Trade Row Ka Role: Kyunki EA ab same-direction re-entries kar sakta hai, Max Trade Row limit (Chapter 6) is design mein aur zaroori ho jata hai — weak/choppy trend periods mein overtrading se bachne ke liye ise reasonable value pe rakho (default 2).
ParameterKya Karta HaiDefault
EMA TimeframeEMA kis timeframe se calculate ho (Current Chart / M15 / M30 / H1 / H4 / D1 etc.)Current Chart

परिचय Overview

इस चैप्टर में हम समझेंगे कि AG Swing + EMA + R:R EA क्या है, और यह कैसे काम करता है।

1. EA क्या है

AG Swing + EMA + R:R EA (Expert Advisor) एक फुल ऑटो-ट्रेडिंग MT5 बॉट है — जो हमारे Swing + EMA लॉजिक के साथ खुद एंट्रीज़ लेता है, SL/TP मैनेज करता है, और पोज़िशन को पूरी तरह ऑटोमैटिक कंट्रोल करता है। यह रियल ट्रेड्स प्लेस करता है, रियल पार्शियल क्लोज़ करता है, और पूरा ट्रेड मैनेजमेंट खुद हैंडल करता है — बिना किसी मैनुअल इंटरवेंशन के।

2. कोर लॉजिक

EA का कोर सिग्नल लॉजिक 2 चीज़ों पर बेस्ड है:

  • EMA — ट्रेंड डायरेक्शन बताने के लिए
  • Swing High / Swing Low ब्रेक — मोमेंटम कन्फर्मेशन के लिए

जब दोनों कंडीशन मैच होती हैं, EA खुद BUY या SELL ऑर्डर प्लेस कर देता है।

EA के फुल फीचर्स: Master Control (On/Off, Only Buy/Sell), 3 Lot Sizing modes (Fixed/% Risk/$ Risk), Real Partial Close (actual lot close होता है), EMA Timeframe (higher timeframe trend filter), और AG Technicals branded dashboard।

3. किसके लिए बना है

उन ट्रेडर्स के लिए जो अपना ट्रेडिंग सिस्टम पूरी तरह ऑटोमेट करना चाहते हैं — बिना मैनुअली चार्ट देखे, बिना मैनुअली ऑर्डर प्लेस किए। EA खुद सिग्नल डिटेक्ट करता है, रिस्क कैलकुलेट करता है, और ट्रेड एग्ज़ीक्यूट करता है।

स्विंग हाई / लो Break Concept

स्विंग डिटेक्ट करने के 2 तरीके — Normal और Basic।

Swing High / Low Break Concept

Two ways to detect swings — Normal and Basic.

1. What Is Swing High / Low

A Swing High is a candle where the candles on both sides are lower (a local peak). A Swing Low is the opposite — candles on both sides are higher.

Swing points are automatically marked on the chart with an "SWH" / "SWL" chip-box

2. Two Calculation Types (ENUM)

TypeHow It Works
NormalUses the Swing Lookback Length parameter — larger = further/more major swings (wider SL)
BasicFixed 2-candle left / 2-candle right classic fractal — more frequent, closer swings (tighter SL)
Which To Choose: If SL looks too wide, try the Basic type — it gives smaller, quicker swings. If you want more reliable/major levels, keep Normal and adjust the Lookback Length.

3. What "Break" Means

A candle's close must cross the previous swing high/low — not the wick. This protects against fake wick spikes.

BUY / SELL Logic Core Signal

When both conditions match, the EA places the order itself.

1. When A Signal Fires

BBUY
1. Candle closes above EMA
2. AND close breaks the previous Swing High
3. → EA immediately places a BUY order
SSELL
1. Candle closes below EMA
2. AND close breaks the previous Swing Low
3. → EA immediately places a SELL order
Separate Magic Numbers: The EA internally uses different magic numbers for BUY and SELL trades — so both directions can be clearly identified/filtered in MT5 history.

2. Chart Visual

On the candle where the signal fires, a green "BUY" or red "SELL" chip-box + arrow symbol appears automatically — no manual marking needed.

Max Trade Control Same Direction

Controlling how many trades in a row are allowed in the same direction.

1. What This Setting Does

In a trending market, several BUY (or SELL) trades can open in a row. This setting controls how many trades in a row are allowed in the same direction, until an opposite signal appears.

This counting is purely condition-match based — it has nothing to do with stoploss.
ParameterWhat It DoesDefault
Enable Max TradeFeature on/offtrue
Max BUY Trades In A RowHow many BUY trades in a row, until a SELL appears2
Max SELL Trades In A RowHow many SELL trades in a row, until a BUY appears2
Why Default 2: The EA is designed to pyramid/re-enter alongside a higher-timeframe EMA trend filter (see Chapter 10).

Stoploss Lock Global Lock

How long a new trade waits for until the current trade closes.

1. What This Feature Does

This is an independent global lock — it works separately from Max Trade Control. When enabled, no new trade opens until the current trade closes.

In the EA, the lock releases once the trade fully closes
OptionBehaviour
Stoploss Hit OnlyA new trade only comes once the current trade fully closes
Stoploss Hit or New SignalTrade closes, or a new valid signal appears (whichever comes first) — lock releases
Keep In Mind: Stoploss Lock and Max Trade Control are both independent.

Risk:Reward + Partial Close Real Trade Management

Real trade management — actual partial position closes happen, not just visuals.

1. How R:R Works

With every trade, Entry, SL, and 3 Take-Profit levels are calculated based on R (Risk) multiples. R = the distance from Entry to SL.

Entry/SL/TP1/TP2/TP3 rays are all drawn on the chart automatically

2. Two TP Modes

ModeHow It Works
Real Partial CloseX% of the position actually closes at TP1, more closes at TP2, the rest at TP3.
Breakeven-TrailNo partial closing — SL moves to breakeven when TP1 is hit, trails to TP1 when TP2 is hit.
Which To Choose: Real Partial Close if you want to lock in profit early. Breakeven-Trail if you want to capture bigger moves with the full position, just making it risk-free early on.

3. Settings

ParameterWhat It DoesDefault
R:R Master EnableSet to false and no TP applies, trade runs on SL onlytrue
TP1/TP2/TP3 R MultipleHow many R away each TP sits1 / 2 / 3
Partial Close Master EnableSet to false and full close only happens at the final TPtrue
TP ModeReal Partial Close / Breakeven-TrailReal Partial Close
TP1/TP2/TP3 Close %How much % closes at each TP in Real Partial mode33 / 33 / 34

EMA Timeframe Multi-Timeframe Trend

Take entries on a small chart, aligned with a bigger timeframe's trend.

1. The Concept

EMA's timeframe can now be set separately — e.g. trade on an M5 chart, but use an H1 EMA as the trend filter.

As long as the bigger trend (H1) holds, multiple entries can fire on the smaller TF (M5)

2. Why It's Useful

  • The higher TF EMA confirms the overall trend — filtering out fake reversals on the smaller TF
  • Even after a TP hits on M5, as long as the H1 trend holds, the EA can re-enter the same direction
  • Captures more of strong trending moves that a single-trade approach would miss
Keep In Mind: Swing High/Low detection always stays on the chart's own timeframe — only the EMA trend filter comes from the higher TF. The higher TF EMA value stays "stale" until its own larger candle closes.
Max Trade Row's Role: Since the EA can now re-enter the same direction, the Max Trade Row limit (Chapter 6) becomes even more important in this design.

Installation MetaTrader 5 Setup

Step-by-step guide to attaching the EA on MT5.

1. Step-by-Step Setup

1Place the file in MetaEditor
In MT5, go to File → Open Data Folder → MQL5 → Experts and paste the AG_Swing_EMA_RR_EA.mq5 file.
2Compile
Open the file in MetaEditor and press F7 (Compile). You should see "0 errors".
3Drag onto a chart
Find the EA under Experts in the MT5 Navigator panel — drag-drop it onto the chart of the pair/timeframe you want.
4Enable AutoTrading
Turn ON the "Algo Trading" button in the top toolbar — otherwise the EA won't take trades.
5Adjust settings
In the dialog box, set your parameters under the Inputs tab as needed.
6Check the Common tab
In the dialog box's Common tab, "Allow Algo Trading" must be checked ON.
Important: The EA needs an internet connection and the MT5 terminal to stay open. Using a VPS is recommended for 24x7 uptime.
Tip: Backtest in Strategy Tester first, or run it on a demo account for a few days, before using it live.
